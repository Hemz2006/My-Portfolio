Journal paper · MDPI · 2026

            
LegalAssist: A Temporally Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for Searching Indian Court Judgments

            

              
Authors
Hemanth Varma Kanumuri, Jakkidi Pradyun, Sreeja Moganty, Rishiraj Bhardwaj, Nidhi Goyal

              
Published in
Engineering Proceedings 2026, Volume 143, Issue 1, 86 (MDPI)

            

            
An open-source system that helps advocates and law students search Indian Supreme Court and High Court judgments and get legal answers backed by real sources. Paid legal databases are expensive, and general-purpose chatbots often invent case law. LegalAssist tackles both with a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline that runs on consumer hardware.

            
    
              
  • Search: combines keyword search (BM25) with meaning-based search (FAISS), then reranks the results and gives more weight to higher courts.
    • 
              
  • Safeguards: checks whether a law is still in force (for example, IPC to Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita), verifies citations, and declines to answer when it isn't confident.
    • 
              
  • Answers: written in the FIRAC format (Facts, Issues, Rule, Application, Conclusion) with GPT-4o-mini or a local Qwen model.
    • 
              
  • Finding: on the IL-TUR benchmark, plain BM25 was the strongest retriever. The main contribution is an open, reproducible pipeline that reduces unsupported legal answers.
    • 
            

            
  • RAG
  • NLP
  • Information retrieval
  • LLMs
  • Legal AI
